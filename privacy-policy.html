Privacy Policy for Atomik Life

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Atomik Life ("the App") is a personal finance management application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses and how it is used.

Information We Access

  • Camera: Used only to scan and capture receipt images for expense tracking.
  • Storage/Media: Used to let you select existing receipt images from your device.

How Your Data Is Used

All financial data you enter or scan is stored locally on your device. Only anonymized usage patterns may be processed to power the in-app financial assistant feature. We do not sell or share your personal financial data with third parties.

Data Retention

Your data remains on your device and under your control. You may delete the app at any time to remove all locally stored data.

Your Rights

You may request information about, or deletion of, any data associated with your use of the App by contacting us at the email below.
